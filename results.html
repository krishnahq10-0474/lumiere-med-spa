Clinical Philosophy

Our Approach to Results

Aesthetic medicine should never announce itself. At Lumière, we take a conservative, phased approach to every treatment plan. Rather than making dramatic changes in a single afternoon, we prefer micro-adjustments that allow your tissues to adapt naturally.

We photograph all patients under standardized clinical lighting at baseline, immediately post-procedure, and at your complimentary two-week follow-up. This objective clinical tracking guarantees your satisfaction and ensures your enhancements harmonize with your facial structure.
