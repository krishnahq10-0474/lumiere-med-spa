Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers regarding comfort, safety, clinical longevity, and appointment policies.

Patient comfort is one of our primary clinical priorities. For injectable treatments, we use medical-grade topical numbing creams, vibration distraction devices, and ultra-fine needles. Most clients describe the feeling as a momentary slight pinch. For laser treatments, our GentleMax Pro incorporates an integrated cryogenic cooling blast that chills the skin before every pulse.
Longevity depends on the specific treatment and individual metabolic rate. Neuromodulators (Botox/Dysport) typically last 3 to 4 months. Dermal fillers last between 9 and 18 months depending on the formula and facial placement. SkinPen microneedling and chemical peel results build cumulatively over a series of sessions, with results sustained through consistent home skincare and quarterly maintenance visits.
Most of our treatments have little to zero social downtime. With Botox and HydraFacial, you may return to work or social events immediately. Dermal fillers may produce mild swelling or localized bruising for 24 to 72 hours. Microneedling typically leaves the skin with mild redness similar to a moderate sunburn for 24 hours. We advise scheduling treatments at least two weeks before major public events.
Every clinical procedure at Lumière is performed exclusively by licensed, board-certified healthcare professionals: our Board-Certified Physician Medical Director, Nurse Practitioners (NP-C), Physician Assistants (PA-C), Registered Nurses (RN), and Licensed Master Aestheticians (LE). We never delegate clinical procedures to unlicensed medical assistants.
Our standard Botox pricing is $13.50 per unit, while Lumière Members enjoy preferred rates starting at $9.75 to $11.00 per unit. A typical treatment area (such as the glabella or crow's feet) usually requires between 15 and 30 units, depending on muscle strength and aesthetic objectives. We provide a fully transparent written quote before any injection is administered.
Yes. Under Arizona medical regulations and our practice safety ethics, a medical clearance and comprehensive assessment must precede any treatment. For new patients who wish to receive treatment on the same day, we schedule sufficient time so your consultation and treatment can happen seamlessly in one visit.
Most healthy adults seeking conservative aesthetic enhancements or skin restoration are great candidates. We do not perform elective cosmetic injections on patients who are pregnant or nursing, or individuals with active neuromuscular disorders or skin infections in the treatment area. During your consultation, we carefully assess all medical factors to confirm safety.
We reserve private treatment suites and dedicated clinical time for each patient. We kindly ask for at least 48 hours notice for any cancellation or rescheduling request. Appointments cancelled with less than 24 hours notice may be subject to a $75 cancellation fee, which can be applied toward a rescheduled treatment within 30 days.
