Practice Background

Our Story & Standards

Founded in Scottsdale by a board-certified physician and an aesthetic nurse practitioner who believed patients deserved medical rigor, honest guidance, and natural results.

Founding Vision

Born From Clinical Ethics and Artistic Restraint

Lumière Aesthetics was established when Dr. Aris Thorne, MD, and Jessica Moore, MSN, NP-C, recognized a troubling gap in the cosmetic medicine landscape: med spas had become high-pressure retail storefronts, pushing fleeting trends and standardized volume treatments that distorted natural facial proportions.

They set out to build an intentional alternative in Scottsdale. A clinical sanctuary where appointments are unhurried, patient safety is supervised on-site by a licensed physician, and every injection honors individual facial anatomy.

At Lumière, we measure clinical success not by how much product we place, but by how refreshed and confident you look when you leave our doors.

Foundational Pillars

Our Core Values

These four principles guide every clinical recommendation and patient interaction.

01 • RIGOR

Medical Rigor Over Trends

We evaluate new aesthetic protocols against peer-reviewed clinical evidence before introducing them to our menu. We never chase unproven social media fads.

02 • RESTRAINT

Anatomical Restraint

The best aesthetic outcome is undetectable. We respect dynamic muscle vectors and bone structure, choosing conservative micro-dosing over excessive volume.

03 • SAFETY

Uncompromising Safety

We only use FDA-approved pharmaceuticals sourced directly from verified US distributors. Our sterile standards meet hospital surgical suite protocols.

04 • HONESTY

Transparent Communication

We provide clear, honest explanations of clinical timelines, potential side effects, and exact costs upfront. If a procedure is not appropriate for you, we will tell you.

Medical Rigor

Clinical Safety & Sterilisation Protocols

Your health and physical safety precede every aesthetic consideration. We follow strict sterilization standards that reflect hospital operating guidelines:

Direct Pharmaceutical Chain

All neurotoxins and dermal fillers are purchased directly from verified US manufacturers (Allergan, Galderma, Merz). We strictly prohibit grey-market imports.

Single-Use Micro-Needles

Every needle, cannula, syringe, and SkinPen cartridge is single-use, pre-sterilized, and discarded in medical biohazard sharps containers immediately after use.

Emergency Readiness

Our facility is fully stocked with ultrasound guidance, high-concentration hyaluronidase (Hylenex), oxygen, and emergency reversal medication at all times.
